About the data

Where the map's information comes from and how to read it.

Source

The map uses data from Global Fishing Watch, which analyses vessel tracking signals to estimate where and when fishing is taking place.

Reading the map

Brighter areas show more apparent fishing activity during the selected dates. Activity is estimated from vessel movement, so it shows likely fishing rather than confirmed catches.

Limitations

Vessels that do not broadcast a tracking signal, or whose signal is weak or switched off, may be missing or under-represented.
